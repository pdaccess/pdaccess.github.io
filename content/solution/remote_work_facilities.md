## Remote Access That Doesn't Compromise Security

The shift to distributed workforces has made remote access a business necessity — but opening corporate systems to remote users is one of the biggest security challenges organizations face. VPNs expose management interfaces. Shared credentials invite insider threats. Unmonitored sessions leave no audit trail when incidents occur.

PDAccess provides a secure, auditable remote access platform that gives employees the connectivity they need while keeping your infrastructure protected, your credentials secret, and your compliance requirements satisfied.

## The Remote Access Problem

Most organizations approach remote access the wrong way: they open firewalls, deploy VPNs, and distribute credentials. This creates a sprawling attack surface that security teams struggle to monitor. When a breach occurs, there's no way to tell which remote user did what, or when.

The alternative — a centralized, proxy-based access model — eliminates the need to expose any systems to the internet while maintaining full access to corporate resources.

## How PDAccess Works

### Proxy-Based Connection Model

Instead of exposing your servers to remote users, PDAccess brings the connection to the user. Engineers download the PDAccess client, authenticate with their enterprise credentials, and connect to authorized services through the platform's encrypted proxy. Your servers never touch the public internet. Your firewall rules stay tight. Your attack surface shrinks.

### Centralized Credential Management

Remote workers don't need to know — or store — any server credentials. When they initiate a session, PDAccess retrieves the credentials from its encrypted vault and injects them automatically. No password sharing. No credential files on laptops. No risk of theft if a device is compromised.

### Granular Access Policies

Not every remote worker needs the same access. PDAccess enforces role-based access controls so that a developer can access development environments, a sysadmin can manage production systems, and a contractor gets time-bound access to a specific application — nothing more.

### Full Session Visibility

Every remote session is recorded. Administrators can review past sessions, monitor active connections, and revoke access instantly. This visibility is critical for compliance audits, incident investigation, and simply knowing who's accessing your systems and when.

## Compliance by Design

Remote access is one of the areas most scrutinized by regulators. PDAccess delivers:

- **PCI-DSS**: Per-user authentication and session recording for all remote access to cardholder data environments
- **SOX**: Audit trails showing exactly who accessed financial systems and what they did
- **HIPAA**: Access logging and revocation controls for protected health information systems
- **GDPR**: Ability to instantly revoke access and demonstrate who accessed what data

## The Bottom Line

Remote access shouldn't be a trade-off between convenience and security. With PDAccess, your distributed workforce gets the connectivity they need, your security team gets the visibility they require, and your compliance team gets the audit evidence they demand — all from a single platform.
