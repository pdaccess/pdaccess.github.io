## Protecting Critical Infrastructure with Zero-Trust Access

Industrial control systems — PLCs, SCADA, DCS, and HMIs — are the backbone of manufacturing, energy, water treatment, and transportation. They're also one of the most attacked and least protected parts of an organization's IT landscape. OT environments were designed for uptime, not security, and traditional PAM solutions rarely support the proprietary protocols these systems depend on.

PDAccess bridges that gap by providing secure, auditable remote access to industrial systems without exposing them to the public internet.

## The Challenge

- **Legacy systems** weren't built for remote access, leaving organizations with risky workarounds: shared credentials, unencrypted tunnels, or physical visits to remote sites.
- **Protocol diversity** means standard remote access tools can't connect to PLCs, SCADA servers, or DCS controllers natively.
- **Compliance pressure** from NIST SP 800-82, IEC 62443, and sector-specific regulations demands per-user accountability and session recording.
- **Third-party risk** — integrators, maintenance contractors, and OEMs need access but shouldn't have full credentials or unrestricted control.

## How PDAccess Solves It

### Secure Proxy-Based Access

PDAccess eliminates the need for firewall rules, VPN tunnels, or exposed management interfaces. Technicians connect through the PDAccess client, which routes sessions through the platform's encrypted proxy. Your OT network stays isolated. Your management interfaces stay closed.

### Multi-Protocol Gateway

PDAccess supports RDP, SSH, and VNC connections — the primary protocols used to manage industrial systems. Whether a technician needs to troubleshoot a PLC through an RDP jump server, access a SCADA HMI via SSH tunnel, or connect to a DCS console, PDAccess handles the connection without the user ever touching the underlying credentials.

### Session Recording & Command Logging

Every session is recorded. Every command is logged. When a maintenance engineer connects to a critical system, PDAccess captures the full session for later review — satisfying audit requirements and providing a forensic trail if something goes wrong.

### Just-in-Time Access for Contractors

Maintenance contractors, OEM technicians, and integrators need access for a defined period. PDAccess creates time-bound access policies that automatically expire. When the work is done, access is revoked — no credential handoff, no shared passwords, no lingering permissions.

## The Business Impact

Organizations using PDAccess for OT/ICS security achieve:

- **Eliminated shared credentials** across industrial sites and maintenance teams
- **Audit-ready session records** that satisfy IEC 62443 and NIST requirements
- **Faster remote troubleshooting** — engineers can resolve issues without traveling to remote sites
- **Reduced operational risk** — no exposed management interfaces, no firewall exceptions, no VPN overhead

## Bottom Line

Critical infrastructure deserves security controls that match its importance. PDAccess brings enterprise-grade privileged access management to OT environments — where traditional solutions fall short — so you can maintain uptime, satisfy regulators, and empower your teams with the access they need without exposing what matters most.
